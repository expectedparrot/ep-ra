# Simulation plans

For stateful simulations, retain the seven-section structure but adapt:

- Section 3 becomes **Entities**: roles, traits, goals, instructions, and
  relevant prefabs.
- Section 4 becomes **Scenario and dynamics**: initial state, game-master or
  environment behavior, events, stopping condition, repetitions, and saved
  logs.
- Section 5 previews the observable trajectory, summary measures, and
  representative dialogue or actions.

Identify whether the design uses an existing game or a custom environment.
Explain what varies across runs and what remains fixed. Treat simulation-run
approval and cost as separate from plan approval.
