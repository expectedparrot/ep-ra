# AI, human, and combined modality review

Explain briefly what the selected modality can and cannot establish for this
goal. Surface only unresolved decisions that could change the design, recommend
a path, and offer a simple default.

## AI simulation

Prioritize coverage of relevant perspectives and experimental conditions before
agent count. Treat outputs as model evidence, not prevalence or observed human
behavior. Suggest a small pilot, repeated runs, or a second model only when
instrument or model sensitivity matters. Load model-selection and cost guidance
before making concrete choices. Do not import human power logic into an AI
persona count.

## Humans

Resolve the target population and whether participants are supplied, reached by
an open/private link, or recruited. When relevant, surface eligibility,
sample/compensation, respondent experience and accessibility, privacy/consent,
preview, response quality, monitoring, and actual cost. Never assume Prolific
and never publish paid recruitment without its required live-filter, cost, and
approval checks.

## External AI agents

When the respondent is an AI agent that runs outside Expected Parrot (the
user's own assistant or agent, or agents participants bring), the survey is
neither an EDSL simulation nor a browser survey. Deploy it as a human survey,
turn on agent access, and share the agent link. Load
`skill:edsl-human-survey-agent-access`. The browser respondent link is the
wrong link for an agent.

## Both

Give the phases distinct roles, normally using AI as a bounded pilot before
humans. Define an advancement rule, identify measures that must remain
comparable, and say explicitly what human evidence adds that simulation cannot.

Do not reopen a supplied panel, model, recruitment source, or modality. Suppress
the review for a narrow edit. Re-run only the newly applicable portion when the
study changes modality.
