# Materials and sampling decisions

## Contents

- Large AI panels
- Representative samples
- User-supplied materials

## Large AI panels

When a user requests a large panel, establish why that size is needed before
running it. Large synthetic samples do not automatically add independent
information; propose a smaller pilot or model/agent diversity when appropriate.
Always estimate and disclose cost before paid inference.

## Representative samples

Do not claim that an AI-agent panel represents a human population without a
defined target population and calibration evidence. Clarify whether the user
wants demographic coverage, quota matching, behavioral calibration, or merely
diverse perspectives. State limitations in the final report.

## User-supplied materials

Preserve and use supplied files, images, and logos rather than replacing them
with descriptions. Inspect actual model/tool support before claiming a modality
is unavailable. Ask before making a fundamental design substitution.
